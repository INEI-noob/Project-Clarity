# Relationships & Dating

&gt; Queer dating is beautiful, complicated, and often terrifying. Let's navigate it together.

## Before You Start

**Know your why:**
- Exploring? (Valid)
- Lonely? (Valid)
- Need validation? (Valid but risky)
- Rebounding? (Be upfront about it)

**Know your limits:**
- Minimum age gaps
- Polyamory vs monogamy
- Physical boundaries
- Emotional availability

**Red flags you might ignore because "queer dating is hard":**
- They're secretive about their life (beyond closet safety)
- All their exes are "crazy"
- Moving too fast (love bombing)
- Guilting you for not being out
- No friends or community

## First Dates: Safety

### Public places only
- Coffee shops, museums, busy parks
- Not their house, not your house, not "secluded spots"

### Tell someone
- Text friend: "Going on date with [name], here's photo, expect check-in at 9pm"
- Share location (Find My Friends, Life360, WhatsApp live location)
- Have "get me out" code word with friend

### The "Are they real?" check
- Video chat before meeting
- Reverse image search their photos
- Check social media exists and has history
- Trust your gut—if photos look too polished, they might be catfishing

### Exit strategies
- "I can only stay an hour, meeting friend after"
- Pre-scheduled "emergency" text from friend
- Pepper spray/legal self-defense if dating while femme-presenting

## Queer-Specific Dynamics

### The "U-Haul" stereotype
Lesbian/queer women relationships sometimes move fast emotionally (and through apartments). It's okay to pump brakes.

### The "Straight Passing" pressure
Dating someone of opposite gender doesn't make you less queer. Dating someone same gender doesn't prove your queerness. Your identity is yours.

### Chasers and fetishization
- **Trans women**: Beware men who obsess over "feminine penis" or want to be "taught"
- **Trans men**: Beware "yaoi fangirls" who want to fetishize you
- **NB folks**: Beware people who treat you as "experimentation"
- **Bi folks**: Beware couples seeking "unicorns" without respecting your full humanity

**You're not a learning experience. You're a person.**

## Healthy Communication

### The queer conversations you need to have:
- **Safer sex**: Different genital configurations = different risk profiles. Dental dams, gloves, PrEP, condoms on toys—discuss it all.
- **Gender affirming**: What pronouns in public vs private? Touching chest/genitals okay or dysphoric triggers?
- **Coming out status**: Who knows about you? About them? Leak risks?
- **Exes/Community**: Small queer circles mean you probably have exes in common. Transparency prevents drama.

### Green flags:
- They ask about your boundaries before assuming
- They introduce you to friends (when safe)
- They respect your "no" without pouting
- They know about queer history/issues beyond just dating
- They have other queer friends (not just romantic interests)

## Heartbreak

**Queer heartbreak hits different:**
- Smaller dating pool = harder to move on
- Mutual friends in tight community = awkwardness
- Identity questioning: "Was I faking? Is that why they left?"
- Fear this was your "one chance" at queer love

**It's not true.** Love is abundant. You will love again. You are lovable.

**Community care:**
- Ask friends to check on you
- Avoid "revenge posting" online
- Don't rebound with their friends (small community etiquette)
