# Understanding Gender

&gt; Gender is not what is between your legs. It is what is between your ears—and your heart.

## The Basics

**Sex** (biological): Chromosomes, hormones, anatomy assigned at birth  
**Gender** (identity): Who you know yourself to be  
**Expression**: How you present yourself to the world

These don't always align. And that's okay.

---

## The Spectrum

Imagine gender not as: Male <--------------------> Female

But as:


**Non-binary**: Identifying outside the male/female binary  
**Genderfluid**: Shifting between identities  
**Agender**: Not identifying with any gender  
**Transgender**: Identifying differently than assigned at birth  
**Cisgender**: Identifying with assigned at birth

**All are valid. All are real. All are enough.**

---

## Questioning Your Gender

**Signs you might be trans or non-binary:**
- Discomfort with pronouns or gendered language directed at you
- Feeling "wrong" in your body, specifically gendered features
- Jealousy of another gender's presentation or social role
- Euphoria when gendered correctly (even accidentally)
- A persistent sense that you're "faking" your assigned gender

**But also:**
- Not all trans people "always knew"
- Not all trans people experience dysphoria
- Not all trans people want to medically transition

**There is no "trans enough."**

## Exploring Safely

### Online
- Try different names/pronouns in online spaces
- Create accounts with different gender presentations
- Join trans/nb Discord servers or forums

### In Private
- Dress in private
- Take photos (don't have to keep them)
- Voice training videos (if desired)
- Journal: "Today I felt like..."

### With Others
- Ask trusted friends to use different pronouns temporarily
- Attend trans/nb support groups (online or local)

---

## Medical Transition (Optional)

**Not all trans people transition medically. Not all who want to can afford it. You are still valid.**

**Social transition**: Name, pronouns, presentation  
**Medical transition**: Hormones, surgeries  
**Legal transition**: Documents, IDs

**In South Africa:**
- Hormone therapy available through public health (long wait) or private
- Gender marker change possible through Department of Home Affairs
- Many surgeons in Gauteng and Western Cape

## Dealing with Doubt

**"What if I'm wrong?"**

Then you tried something brave and learned about yourself. Cis people experiment with gender presentation too. There is no failure in exploration.

**"What if I'm too old?"**

You're not. Transition (social or medical) happens at every age. The "right time" is when you're ready.

**"What if I lose everything?"**

That's a real fear. Community can help you prepare. You don't have to rush. Survival first, authenticity second—though ideally, eventually, both.
