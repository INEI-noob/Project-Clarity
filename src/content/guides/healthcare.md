# Navigating Healthcare

&gt; You deserve affirming care. Here's how to find it—and survive when you can't.

## Finding Affirming Providers

### In South Africa

**GPs who are safe:**
- Call first: "Do you have experience with LGBTQ+ patients?" (Gauge reaction)
- Look for: Rainbow flag stickers, inclusive intake forms
- Ask community: "Who is your trans-friendly doctor?" in local groups

**Specific needs:**

**HRT (Hormone Replacement Therapy):**
- Public: Groote Schuur, Charlotte Maxeke have gender clinics (long waits)
- Private: Endocrinologists in Sandton, Cape Town CBD
- Informed consent: Some GPs will prescribe without therapist letter

**PrEP (HIV prevention):**
- Available free at government clinics
- Ask for: "I'm interested in PrEP for HIV prevention"
- No need to disclose sexuality but helpful if you're comfortable

**Therapy:**
- Psychology Today has "LGBTQ+" filter for SA therapists
- Ask: "Do you follow WPATH standards?" (certifies gender competency)
- Red flag: Therapist wants to "cure" you or sees queerness as trauma symptom

### Bad Healthcare: How to Survive

**If misgendered repeatedly:**
- "My pronouns are ___. Please chart that."
- "I'm here for [flu/stomach issue], not my gender. Can we focus on that?"
- Bring advocate friend if possible

**If refused care:**
- Ask for reason in writing (they often back down)
- File complaint with HPCSA (Health Professions Council)
- Find LGBTQ+ health advocacy org in your province

## Mental Health Specifics

**Queer-competent therapists ask:**
- About family acceptance, not just symptoms
- About minority stress (discrimination impact on mental health)
- About chosen family support systems

**Red flags (leave immediately):**
- "Have you tried not being gay/trans?"
- Religious framing of your identity
- Focus on your childhood "causing" queerness
- Encouraging you to come out before you're ready

## Sexual Health

**Testing without judgment:**
- Every 3 months if sexually active with multiple partners
- Every 6 months if monogamous
- Services: free at most clinics, or Dis-Chem/Clicks for privacy

**What to ask for:**
- HIV (4th gen test)
- Syphilis
- Hepatitis B/C
- Chlamydia & Gonorrhea (urine or swab)
- If receptive anal sex: anal Pap smear (yes, it's a thing)

**PEP (Post-Exposure Prophylaxis):**
- After condomless sex with potentially HIV+ person
- Must start within 72 hours (sooner = better)
- Side effects are rough but temporary

## Insurance & Privacy

**Medical aid in SA:**
- Discovery, Bonitas, etc. don't disclose specifics to principal members (if you're dependent)
- But explanation of benefits might show "endocrinology" or "psychiatry"
- Solution: Pay cash for sensitive visits, claim general practitioner code

**Parental access:**
- Once you're 16+, medical info is private in SA
- If parents call doctor, doctor legally can't share (unless you're danger to self/others)

## Emergency: Bad Reaction to Meds

**If DIY HRT or hormones:**
- Deep vein thrombosis (blood clot) symptoms: leg pain, chest pain, shortness of breath → ER immediately
- Liver issues: yellowing skin, extreme fatigue → ER

**If you can't afford care:**
- Medicross, Intercare offer payment plans
- Township clinics are free but often not affirming—bring advocate
- Hospital social workers can help with indigent programs

You deserve care that sees your whole self, not just your symptoms.
