# Religion & Spirituality

&gt; You can be queer and spiritual. Yes, really.

## The Wound

Many of us were raised in faiths that taught us:
- We're abominations
- We're broken
- We'll burn in hell
- God can't love us as we are

**This is spiritual abuse.** It's not true, and it's not your fault.

## Reclaiming Faith

### Christianity

**Affirming theology:**
- **Matthew Vines**: "God and the Gay Christian" 
- **Rev. Dr. Cheryl Townsend**: Womanist theology
- **Side A vs Side B**: Side A = affirming; Side B = celibacy but not "change"

**Affirming churches in SA:**
- **Methodist Church of Southern Africa**: Officially allows same-sex marriage (2020)
- **Anglican Church of Southern Africa**: Mixed but some parishes affirming
- **Inclusive and Affirming Ministries (IAM)**: iam.org.za

**Distinction:**
- **Conservative churches**: Focus on "sin," conversion, celibacy
- **Affirming churches**: Focus on love, justice, incarnation

### Islam

**Progressive Muslims:**
- **Irshad Manji**: "The Trouble with Islam Today"
- **MPV (Muslims for Progressive Values)**: mpvusa.org
- Local: Cape Town has queer Muslim groups

**The Quran:**
- Story of Lut interpreted as about rape/violence, not homosexuality by progressive scholars
- "Allah made diversity" (Quran 49:13)

### Traditional African Spirituality

**Before colonization:**
- Many African cultures recognized gender diversity
- Sangomas can be same-sex loving
- Ancestral lineage isn't broken by queerness (genes aren't the only lineage)

**Reclaiming:**
- Sangoma/traditional healer acceptance varies—find queer-affirming ones
- Ancestors want your wholeness, not your conformity

### Secular Spirituality

**You don't need religion to be spiritual:**
- Meditation (Headspace, Insight Timer have queer-focused sessions)
- Nature connection
- Ancestor veneration without religious dogma
- Ethics based on harm reduction, consent, and care

## When Family Uses Religion Against You

**"The Bible says..." responses:**
- "The Bible also says shellfish is an abomination. Do you eat prawns?"
- "I interpret Genesis 1:31 ('God saw all creation was good') to include me."
- "The Bible has been used to justify slavery and apartheid. Interpretation evolves."

**"You'll go to hell" responses:**
- "If heaven is full of homophobes, I'd rather be in hell with the queer kids."
- "God made me this way. Why would he damn his own creation?"

**Setting boundaries:**
- "We can disagree on theology, but you cannot use my identity as a weapon."
- "If you can't respect my humanity, we can't have this relationship."

## Deconstruction

**Steps away from toxic faith:**
1. **Question**: "Do I believe this, or was I taught this?"
2. **Grieve**: You may need to mourn the safety religion promised
3. **Rebuild**: What values do YOU hold? (Probably: kindness, justice, love)
4. **Community**: Find spiritual or secular community that affirms you

**It's okay to leave religion entirely.** You don't owe anyone your participation in spaces that harm you.
