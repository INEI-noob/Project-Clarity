# Digital Safety

&gt; Your digital footprint can out you before you're ready. Protect yourself.

## The Risks

**Digital traces persist:**
- Browser history
- Search queries
- Location data
- Screenshots of apps/grids
- Social media likes/follows
- Shared devices

**Who might look:**
- Parents checking phones
- Partners suspicious of cheating
- Employers (legally grey but happens)
- Governments (depending on where you live)

## Immediate Protection

### Browser Level
- **Use incognito/private mode** for all LGBTQ+ searches
- **Clear history regularly** (Ctrl+Shift+Delete)
- **Use DuckDuckGo** instead of Google (no tracking)
- **Browser compartmentalization**: Firefox for "straight" stuff, Brave for queer stuff

### Device Level
- **App locks**: Lock specific apps (Grindr, Tinder, Discord) with PIN/biometrics
- **Fake calculator apps**: Hide photos in disguised apps
- **Second user profile**: Android supports multiple users completely separated
- **Never use fingerprint unlock** if parents/partners might force your hand while sleeping

### Social Media
- **Close friends lists**: Post queer content only to trusted circles
- **Remove location tags**: Especially from queer venues
- **Archive vs Delete**: Instagram archive hides from public but keeps for you
- **Alt accounts**: Completely separate emails, phone numbers (Google Voice), profiles

## The Big Apps

### Grindr/Tinder/Her
- **Disable distance**: Shows exact location
- **No face pics** until verified safe
- **Screenshot notifications**: Some apps tell users when screenshotted
- **Block &gt; Report**: Blocking is instant, reporting might notify them first

### Discord/Telegram
- **Disappearing messages**: Set 24hr auto-delete in sensitive chats
- **No real name/username**: Don't reuse usernames across platforms
- **Separate account entirely** from "straight" Discord

### Instagram/TikTok
- **Private accounts** for queer content
- **Remove from suggested**: Settings &gt; Privacy &gt; Account Center &gt; Manage location
- **Don't sync contacts**: Prevents "people you may know" suggestions

## Advanced: If You're Technical

- **VPN**: ProtonVPN (free tier, Swiss privacy laws)
- **Tor Browser**: For research without any tracking
- **Separate devices**: Old phone for queer apps only, keep at trusted friend's house
- ** burner emails**: SimpleLogin or AnonAddy for every service

## If You Get Outed

**Screenshots as evidence:**
- Document harassment
- Save threatening messages
- Don't delete until you're safe

**Emergency cleanup:**
- "Find My Device" remote wipe if phone seized
- Pre-written "I was hacked" excuse (not great, but sometimes necessary for survival)
- Emergency contact who knows truth and can help

## Remember

**Digital safety is real safety.** In some countries, queerness is tracked digitally and prosecuted. In conservative homes, digital discovery leads to homelessness or conversion therapy.

**Be paranoid early.** It's easier to relax later than to recover from being outed.

**Trust your gut.** If someone asks too many questions about your phone, they suspect something.
